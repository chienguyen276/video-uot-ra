## Style Prompt
Warm, gentle, cute Vietnamese roomtour. Full-frame vertical footage with small scrapbook note cards. Calm cross-dissolves, restrained floating labels, generous breathing room. User requested nhẹ nhàng and cute.
## Colors
- #FFF8EB cream note paper
- #354B40 forest green ink
- #D8E4CF sage accents
- #DDAE99 blush tape
## Typography
Noto Sans for Vietnamese notes; Noto Serif for display headings.
## What NOT to Do
No rapid flashes. No large graphics covering amenities. No invented price, area or facilities. No aggressive zooms.
